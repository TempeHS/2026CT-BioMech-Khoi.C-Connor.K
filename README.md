# Aple Healthcare

Our patient is Vivian, who has a coronary artery abnomarlity which means she her heart was formed abnormally at birth. She requires a device that will monitor her heart rate and send alerts when their heart rate gets too high. Our system will help the patient by monitoring their exertion to prevent injury.
